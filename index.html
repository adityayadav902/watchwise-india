How it works

Transparent ranking, honest data

1

Search with an India focus

The backend queries YouTube with regionCode=IN, your category and your description.

2

Fetch real statistics

For each candidate it reads views, likes, comments, duration and publish date from the YouTube Data API.

3

Score and cache

Videos are scored with the weights below, then the ranking is cached and refreshed every 20 days.

Ranking criteria

    Shorts, live streams and videos under 90 seconds are skipped, and no channel appears more than twice. Hidden like counts are simply not counted. The ranking reflects data available at the last refresh and the selected criteria.
